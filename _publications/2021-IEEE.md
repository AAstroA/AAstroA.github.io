  <section class="publication-detail-modern__summary">
    <p class="section-kicker">Research summary</p>
    <p>In distributed algorithms, processing nodes communicate with one another to reach a common objective, but noisy or malfunctioning nodes can distort the information shared across the network. This paper develops a method that adapts combination weights according to the recent performance of neighboring nodes rather than relying on a fixed weighting rule.</p>
    <p>Applied to diffusion LMS algorithms, the approach reduces the influence of impaired nodes and remains robust when network conditions change. The result is a flexible interference-cancellation mechanism for distributed systems in which the reliability of individual agents can vary over time.</p>
